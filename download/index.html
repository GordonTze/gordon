Quick Reference

Keyboard & Mouse

  • ClickClick a numbered button or a flag on the map to fly to that checkpoint.
  • DragDrag the map to orbit around the terrain.
  • ScrollScroll to zoom in and out.
  • 1–8Jump straight to checkpoint N.
  • ← → / A DNavigate to the previous / next checkpoint.
  • Space / ↑ / WOpen the selected checkpoint's chapter panel.
  • ↓ / SClose the chapter panel.
  • ?Toggle this help window.
  • EscClose the chapter panel, or reset the camera to the intro view.
